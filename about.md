# Panceta Levels List

Browse the **Panceta Levels List** right inside Geometry Dash.

- **PLL** – every level from the PLL+ demon list (main list #1–99, extended, and the UPLL-only levels), with points, List%, victors and a link to the video.
- **GPLL** – coming soon.

Press **View** on any level to open it in the level browser, or the YouTube icon to watch the showcase.

You'll find the **PLL** button on the main menu and on the Create/online page.

Data: [pll-plus.levelslist.workers.dev](https://pll-plus.levelslist.workers.dev/list)
